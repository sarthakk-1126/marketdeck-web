Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 October 2026

MarketDeck provides market research, screening, charting, portfolio, educational and messaging tools. This policy explains how MarketDeck handles personal information when you use marketdeck.in and the MarketDeck WhatsApp research assistant.

Information we may process

  • Information you provide, such as account details, preferences, support messages, and information entered into features you choose to use.
  • Technical and usage information, such as device/browser information, pages visited, referral information and analytics events.
  • For the WhatsApp research assistant, Meta supplies the sender identifier and message/event data needed to process the request and deliver a reply.

Optional first-party product analytics

On instrumented MarketDeck product pages you can allow or decline first-party analytics using the Analytics preferences control. If you allow, we measure page categories, active time, scroll depth, device-size and referral categories, and feature actions. When signed in, these visits can be linked to your MarketDeck account and viewed by authorized team administrators to improve the product and support onboarding.

We do not collect financial inputs, portfolio or wallet values, uploaded files, searches or private prompts, form contents, page titles, raw URLs, IP addresses or browser fingerprints through this first-party collector. Account-linked timeline access is audited. Raw visits and interactions are retained for 90 days and deleted on a daily expiry schedule. Browser identifiers are random; a session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.

You can change your browser choice at any time through Analytics preferences. Declining stops future first-party collection and clears its browser identifiers. It does not delete previous activity; contact support@marketdeck.in for a verified deletion request. Signed-in account deletion removes linked product activity. We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control for this first-party collection. Public-page Google Analytics is a separate existing measurement system.

WhatsApp research assistant

The WhatsApp assistant is designed for read-only research and education. In its production research database, a raw phone number is replaced by a keyed hash where that is sufficient. Inbound message text is processed to answer the request but is not retained as conversation history.

We may retain limited operational state needed to provide the service safely, including session preferences, consent and STOP/delete suppression state, replay/idempotency hashes, short-lived action tokens, rate-limit state, queued delivery state and delivery receipts. This state is bounded and is not used to build a personal investment profile.

How we use information

  • Provide and secure MarketDeck services and requested research features.
  • Maintain preferences, consent, privacy controls and service-window rules.
  • Prevent abuse, duplicate processing and unauthorized access.
  • Measure reliability and improve product performance.
  • Respond to support, legal and security requests.

Data sharing

MarketDeck does not sell personal data. We may use infrastructure and service providers only as needed to operate the service, such as Meta/WhatsApp for messaging, Vercel for application hosting, Neon/PostgreSQL for durable application state, and analytics providers for website measurement. These providers process data under their own applicable terms and privacy obligations.

Market and research data

MarketDeck may show market/company information obtained from MarketDeck systems and public or licensed sources. This information is separate from personal information and is provided for research and educational use, not personalized investment advice.

Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to security, fraud-prevention, legal and operational requirements. WhatsApp users can type STOP to pause research replies and can use DELETE DATA followed by confirmation to delete local research state. A minimal keyed suppression/replay record may be retained where necessary to honor a deletion or do-not-contact request and prevent replay abuse.

See the Data Deletion Instructions for other deletion requests.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards including restricted credentials, signed webhook verification, tenant validation, access controls, bounded data flows and encrypted transport. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can stop WhatsApp research replies at any time with STOP. You may also request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting us. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Children

MarketDeck is not designed for children and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as MarketDeck changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
