## 4. Final question

When do Historical, Gaussian/Parametric and Cornish-Fisher VaR disagree, and does a larger loss threshold imply better independently held-out breach calibration?

## 5. Simulation design

Four controlled iid processes × three estimation lengths × two confidence levels × three methods. 8,000 independent pairs per process/estimation-length condition; 96,000 estimation/holdout pairs total. 576,000 method/confidence thresholds; 264,576,000 generated scalar return observations. All theoretical means zero and standard deviations 1% per synthetic observation. Samples are not normalized to force realized moments.

## 6. Random seed strategy

Master 20261004; NumPy PCG64 with SeedSequence([master, scenario_index, train_length, replication_index, role]). Scenario indices 0–3, replicate 0–7999, role 0 estimation / 1 holdout. Methods/confidences share the pair, with no additional random draws. Different sample lengths are independent streams, not nested histories.

## 7. Distributions and parameters

- Normal: 0.01 × N(0,1).
- Negative skew: skew-normal α = −5, δ = −5/√26; X = δ|Z1| + √(1−δ²)Z2; center by δ√(2/π), scale by √(1−2δ²/π), multiply by 0.01.
- Fat tails: 0.01 × √(6/8) × Student t8; population excess kurtosis 1.5. Fourth moment exists, eighth does not.
- Skew and fat tails: iid mixture 95% N(0,1), 5% N(−4,2²); return = 0.01(X+0.2)/√1.91. Not a persistent volatility regime or market-calibrated shock process.

## 8. Estimation lengths

252, 756, 1260 observations. One/three/five trading years is only an intuitive analogy; no generated observation is an actual trading day. 95% and 99% confidence.

## 9. Holdout methodology

2,000 newly generated observations independent of estimation, from the identical stated process. Breach iff return < −VaR, strictly. Breach targets 5% / 1%, corresponding to 100 / 20 expected holdout breaches for a known correct quantile. Paired methods share holdout observations.

## 10. Formulas and numerical rules

Historical: minus empirical lower-tail quantile, rank (n−1)(1−c), linear interpolation. Gaussian: −(mean + z × sample standard deviation), ddof=1. Central moments divide by n; S=m3/m2^1.5, K=m4/m2²−3. Cornish-Fisher: zCF=z+(z²−1)S/6+(z³−3z)K/24−(2z³−5z)S²/36; threshold=−(mean+zCF×sd). Return fractions are multiplied by 100 once for display.

Kupiec: LR=2[(T−x)log((1−x/T)/(1−p))+x log((x/T)/p)], p=1−c, 0 log0=0; p-value=erfc(√(LR/2)); reject <0.05. Calls frozen product implementation directly. Vectorized VaR proves equivalence to deployed pure functions at rtol1e−12/atol1e−14. Reference StockProof revision `54da2757850dd520ab9c0734a7689bba2dfce41b`; source hash `7d5047b56e895688c14545ec74bcd405f56e83235c082d615f4ce48474386b2b`.

No clipping, winsorization, smoothing, quantile rearrangement or silent exclusion. Nonfinite result fails publication; constant-sample CF is unavailable. CF derivative checked over lower-tail probabilities0.001–0.1; confidence ordering separately checked. All finite estimates kept.
