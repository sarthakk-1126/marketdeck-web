MARKETDECK INTELLIGENCE / LEARNING NOTE

A chart is the start of a better question.

Bring time, context, and a clear purpose to the way you explore price history.

A chart gives you a visual way to explore price history. Start by deciding what you want to understand and which time period is relevant to that question.

Set the frame

Identify the instrument, interval, and date range before interpreting the picture. Make those choices explicit in your research notes so you can return to the same view.

Keep the context

Use charts alongside business research and the wider market conversation. An interesting visual pattern can become a question for further investigation.

Document your thinking

Separate what you observed from what you inferred. MarketDeck Charting provides a dedicated lens for exploring price history and technical context.

The artwork is illustrative and does not represent market data. This note is educational, not a trading recommendation.
