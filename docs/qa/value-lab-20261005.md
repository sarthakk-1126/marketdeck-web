# Value Lab redesign — 5 October 2026

Value Lab now uses the full terminal stage. A readable forecast and per-share
value bridge sit beside compact assumptions; the evidence inspector opens as
an optional drawer. Other views, engine links and owner-scoped saving retain
their existing behavior.

## Data and calculation review

- Filing references come from the latest stored annual period, with reporting
  basis, source traces, publication and retrieval dates. They do not pretend to
  be current market quotes or follow a different selected research period.
- Live verification found older TCS periods with stored zero EPS alongside
  positive attributable profit. These references now require verification; the
  valuation history shows inspectable gaps and the source dialog retains the
  raw stored zero and its explanation. Storage and other research views are
  unchanged. Genuine zero cash/debt figures remain zero.
- Fresh desks start from filed diluted EPS when available. Saved assumptions
  are retained. A three-year average requires consecutive annual periods with
  matching known basis and statement format; its component filings are visible.
- Stored cash-flow statements provide operating cash flow, but not a complete
  verified FCFF/FCFE series. CFO is labelled separately and never used as FCFF.
  The FCFF helper uses reviewed EBIT, tax, depreciation, capex and change in
  non-cash working capital. Unsupported inputs stay empty.
- Rupee totals are converted to crore once. Missing cash or debt components do
  not become zero. The implied annual profit/EPS share count is explicitly an
  approximate weighted average requiring a separate use-as-estimate action.
- Aggregate cash dividends paid are not declared dividends per share.
- All four valuation models retain their existing deterministic calculations.
  The visible bridge reconciles forecast PV, terminal/exit PV and, for firm DCF,
  cash, debt and diluted shares. Invalid terminal growth is rejected.
- Scenario overlays contain only the user's valid same-model cases. Their
  range is labelled as scenario outcomes, not a statistical confidence interval.
