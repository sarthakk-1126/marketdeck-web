# Homepage asset provenance

Both raster illustrations were created for this homepage with the built-in Image Gen tool. They are original editorial illustrations, not satellite measurements or documentary photographs. The supplied screenshots and video were used as visual references only; no source screenshots or video are shipped. No personal information from the supplied product screenshots is included.

The generated PNG masters remain in the local Codex image output directory. Optimized WebP derivatives are committed in the website itself:

- `public/assets/india-earth.webp`: 1920 × 1201; 274,320 bytes.
- `public/assets/india-earth-small.webp`: 1000 × 625; 88,828 bytes. Mobile hero and editorial reuse.
- `public/assets/mumbai-exchange.webp`: 900 × 600; 90,008 bytes.

The local sharp runtime resized and compressed the images without changing their content. No runtime image service is required. Icons, the community orbit, and illustrative research diagrams are code-native SVG. Financial illustrations are explicitly labelled; they do not contain fabricated market values. The wordmark is rendered as text with a simple bar mark inspired by the supplied brand references. The type stack uses local system fonts, with no external font requests.

## Earth generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: high resolution widescreen homepage hero background, 2560 by 1600 composition.
Primary request: an exceptionally premium photorealistic orbital photograph of Earth at night with India front and center, cinematic NASA-quality realism for a serious Indian financial intelligence platform.
Composition: planet fills the RIGHT TWO THIRDS of the image, globe center at x=72% y=49%, complete curved upper horizon visible, globe reaching almost to top and bottom, far right side gently cropped. LEFT 35% is near black empty negative space blending invisibly to #070b10 for website headline overlay. The Earth should feel large and three-dimensional, viewed from space above the Indian Ocean so India, Himalayas, Arabian peninsula and southeast Asia are geographically recognizable. Detailed blue-grey cloud wisps and tactile mountain terrain, restrained warm golden city lights, delicate cool white atmospheric rim illuminated from upper right. India is the focal point with subtly brighter cyan-white city lights and a FEW exquisite hairline communications arcs linking Indian cities. Very sophisticated realistic photographic space, almost black graphite oceans and space with no obvious stars. Rich but restrained neutral palette, no oversaturated blue wash. Crisp land detail, deep realistic spherical shadow at lower left. Expensive editorial design, understated cinematic lighting.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no numbers, no UI, no cards, no fake chart, no watermark, no neon border tracing India, no colorful cartoon globe, no excessive sci-fi beams. Single Earth with accurate geography.

## Editorial generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium financial editorial website thumbnail, landscape 3:2. A beautiful architectural photograph of the Bombay Stock Exchange building in Mumbai, recognizable curved cream tower seen from a low angle with quiet heritage architecture in the foreground, late afternoon warm sunlight and a desaturated charcoal teal sky. Serious sophisticated financial publication, authentic India, tactile limestone and urban detail, subtle film grain. Balanced asymmetrical composition with the rounded stock exchange tower at left-center and sky negative space on right. No people close up, no readable signage, no text, no charts, no logos, no watermarks, no saturated filters. Crisp high end architectural photography.
