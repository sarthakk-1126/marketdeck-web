Data Deletion Instructions

Updated: 2 October 2026

You can request deletion of personal information associated with MarketDeck using the methods below.

WhatsApp research assistant

  1. Open your private chat with the MarketDeck WhatsApp assistant.
  2. Send DELETE DATA.
  3. Complete the explicit Confirm deletion step shown by the assistant.

After confirmation, MarketDeck deletes local research preferences, session state, action tokens and captured replies associated with that user. Research replies remain paused after deletion. A minimal keyed do-not-contact/suppression record and replay-protection record may be retained where necessary to honor the deletion, prevent unwanted replies and defend against duplicate/replayed requests.

Sending STOP pauses research replies without deleting all local research state.

Website/account deletion requests

If you want MarketDeck to delete personal information associated with another MarketDeck feature or account, email support@marketdeck.in with the subject Data Deletion Request. Describe the MarketDeck feature/account involved and provide enough information for us to locate and verify the request. Do not send passwords, access tokens or other secrets.

What happens next

We may ask for reasonable verification before deleting data. We will remove eligible data from active systems within a reasonable period, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, backup and operational requirements. Information that must be retained to comply with law or enforce a verified privacy/suppression request may be kept only for that limited purpose.

Third-party data

Deleting MarketDeck data does not automatically delete information held independently by Meta/WhatsApp, analytics providers, or other third-party services. Requests concerning those services should also be made through their own privacy controls where applicable.
