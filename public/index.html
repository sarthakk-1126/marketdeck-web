FUNDAMENTALS LEARNING NOTE / 01

Begin with the business
behind the stock.

A framework for asking better questions before you open a company’s financials.

Read the perspective

A ticker is a starting point. A useful research process begins with understanding what the company does, how it earns revenue, and the questions you want its filings to answer.

Start with a question

Write down what you want to understand about the business. Where does its revenue come from? What resources does it need to operate? What has management said about its priorities?

Follow the source

Keep the underlying filing close to your notes. Record the reporting period, definitions, and any qualifications alongside the figures you use. This makes it easier to revisit an interpretation later.

Add perspective

Compare the business with its own history and with relevant peers. Use differences as prompts for further investigation. StockProof is the fundamental research lens in the MarketDeck suite.

A research workflow introduction for education. This is not an investment recommendation.

CHARTING LEARNING NOTE / 02

A chart is the start
of a better question.

Bring time, context, and a clear purpose to the way you explore price history.

Read the perspective

A chart gives you a visual way to explore price history. Start by deciding what you want to understand and which time period is relevant to that question.

Set the frame

Identify the instrument, interval, and date range before interpreting the picture. Make those choices explicit in your research notes so you can return to the same view.

Keep the context

Use charts alongside business research and the wider market conversation. An interesting visual pattern can become a question for further investigation.

Document your thinking

Separate what you observed from what you inferred. MarketDeck Charting provides a dedicated lens for exploring price history and technical context.

The artwork is illustrative and does not represent market data. This note is educational, not a trading recommendation.
