MARKETDECK INTELLIGENCE / LEARNING NOTE

Five lenses. One research process.

How different market perspectives can make your next research session more useful.

Different questions call for different tools. MarketDeck brings five research perspectives into one product family so you can choose the lens that suits your task.

Understand the business and the price

Start with company research in StockProof, then explore price history and technical context in Charting. Each perspective can suggest questions to take back to the other.

Explore structure and conversation

F&O is the derivatives lens. Commentary is the lens for market narratives and research context. Use them when those questions are relevant to your process.

Widen the view

Crypto World adds an India-aware perspective on digital assets. The aim across the suite is consistent: create room for more considered research.
