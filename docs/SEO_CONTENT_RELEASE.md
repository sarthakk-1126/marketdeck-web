## Research boundary

Ten relevant Indian/global public education and research pages were inspected for observable titles, headings, topic organisation, examples and links. This was not an authenticated traffic audit. No Search Console property, Ahrefs, Semrush or proprietary keyword-volume dataset was accessed. We cannot identify a publisher's highest-performing pages from public content alone. Competition and opportunity labels are qualitative editorial assessments, not measured keyword difficulty, rank or traffic forecasts.

Primary sources support the article explanations. Competitors inform topic selection and presentation, not copied prose. Several academic sources were reviewed at abstract level and are explicitly labelled that way. DefiLlama definitions were available through indexed public extracts; the article discloses that narrower access rather than claiming a full direct-page review.

## Distinctive contribution and SEO implementation

The editorial position is India-first research that exposes the denominator, timestamp and assumption behind a conclusion. Every guide contains a direct opening answer, substantial original explanation, a worked example or conceptual diagram, linked sources, three reader FAQs, a contents navigation and contextual related/tool links.

The three old reading URLs are retained. The article renderer emits unique titles/descriptions, one H1, H2/H3 sections, self-canonicals, Open Graph metadata, `Article` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD. The publisher/author is MarketDeck as an organisation; no imaginary human analyst, professional credential, independent replication or review board is asserted. An editorial-policy page explains AI assistance, source scopes, dates and limitations.

No FAQPage or HowTo rich-result promise is made. Google's current documentation states FAQ rich results stopped appearing from 7 May 2026, and the FAQ documentation was removed in June. Reader FAQs remain useful content. No schema is a ranking guarantee. We do not change the existing PDF's canonical HTTP headers or claim that work has been done.

The seven new guides are HTML-only. Their cards do not display nonexistent PDF downloads. The existing 12-page magazine retains its real HTML/PDF actions. Draft Research Foundations remains excluded. No category archive is invented just to create extra URLs; current filters serve real entries through the existing hub.

## Maintenance and release controls

Manuscripts: `content/articles/*.md`. Metadata: `content/articles/metadata.json`; integration helper: `scripts/setup-seo-foundations.py`; rendering: `scripts/articles.mjs` within the existing editorial build. Diagrams are original SVGs with text descriptions. All hypothetical examples are identified; real filing headers are linked and limited to the fields actually used.

Future changes should edit metadata intentionally rather than repeatedly running the one-time integration helper, which represents this release's approved inventory. New content needs source checking, a distinct search intent, a substantive article, and explicit publication metadata. The helper does not fetch sources or schedule publication.

Before publishing: build production and review outputs, run `node --test --test-concurrency=1 tests/*.test.mjs`, run the preservation check, and perform HTTP browser/accessibility tests. The preservation guard protects the existing globe, homepage styling/motion, product artwork, magazine/PDF bytes and deployment configuration. The only homepage content change is within the editorial shelf.

After publishing: verify all article URLs, canonical metadata, CSS and diagram bytes over certificate-verified HTTPS. Keep the difference between availability and Google indexing explicit. No Search Console submission, confirmed Google indexing, ranking improvement, organic traffic increase or conversions are claimed by the deployment result.

Review search evidence after publication rather than assuming the plan is correct forever. Check indexed URLs, query impressions, non-brand clicks, landing-page behaviour and meaningful use of related tools where consented measurement exists. A low-traffic article may need a better intent match or distribution, not more repeated keywords. Substantive updates should preserve the URL and document the changed evidence.
