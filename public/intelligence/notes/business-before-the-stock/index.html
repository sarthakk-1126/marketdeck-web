MARKETDECK INTELLIGENCE / LEARNING NOTE

Begin with the business behind the stock.

A framework for asking better questions before you open a company’s financials.

A ticker is a starting point. A useful research process begins with understanding what the company does, how it earns revenue, and the questions you want its filings to answer.

Start with a question

Write down what you want to understand about the business. Where does its revenue come from? What resources does it need to operate? What has management said about its priorities?

Follow the source

Keep the underlying filing close to your notes. Record the reporting period, definitions, and any qualifications alongside the figures you use. This makes it easier to revisit an interpretation later.

Add perspective

Compare the business with its own history and with relevant peers. Use differences as prompts for further investigation. StockProof is the fundamental research lens in the MarketDeck suite.

A research workflow introduction for education. This is not an investment recommendation.
